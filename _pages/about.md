I am a Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) researcher, interaction designer, and developer specializing in VR/XR, spatial interaction, tangible interfaces, and emerging technologies. My work combines user research, experimental design, and rapid prototyping to design and evaluate new ways for people to interact with digital and physical systems.

I am currently seeking opportunities in UX Research, Design Research, Human Factors, XR/VR, Research Scientist, Faculty, and emerging technology roles.


### Highlights

* PhD in Human-Computer Interaction, University of Waterloo
* Published in leading HCI venues including ACM CHI, Graphics Interface (GI), MobileHCI, and SUI
* 10+ years of experience conducting user studies, experimental research, and interactive system prototyping
* Expertise in VR, spatial interaction, world-in-miniature systems, wearable computing, and tangible user interfaces
* Technical background in Unity, C#, rapid prototyping, and interactive system development


### About

I completed my PhD in Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Waterloo, where I worked with Dr. Mark Hancock as a member of Touchlab and the Games Institute. My research explored how physical and virtual worlds can be combined to support more effective interaction techniques, particularly through tangible world-in-miniature systems and immersive environments. Prior to Waterloo, I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Manitoba, where I worked with Dr. Pourang Irani.
