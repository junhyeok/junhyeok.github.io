# About Me
I am a PhD Candidate in Human-Computer Interaction at the **University of Waterloo**, supervised by Dr. Mark Hancock. I am set to defend my dissertation in **April 2026**.

My research focuses on **Virtual Reality (VR)** and **Haptic Feedback**, specifically developing Tangible World-in-Miniature. I have extensive technical experience in **C#** and **Unity** for building immersive environments.

## Research Interests
* Virtual Reality & Augmented Reality
* Haptic Interfaces
* Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
